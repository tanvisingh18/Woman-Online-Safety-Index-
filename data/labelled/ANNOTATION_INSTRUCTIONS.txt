HUMAN VALIDATION — required for thesis error bars
================================================

File: validation_sample.csv (~400 rows from live harsh scrapes)

For each row, set:
  human_label_gendered_harm  →  0 (not gendered harm) or 1 (gendered harm toward women)
  human_label_role           →  perpetrator_attack | victim_disclosure | neutral_discourse
  annotator_notes            →  optional free text

When finished, run:
  python src/validate_classifier.py --import-annotations data/labelled/validation_sample.csv

This produces real precision/recall in outputs/results/classifier_validation.json
under "human_scrape_validation".

Do NOT use --auto-confirm for thesis — that copies model labels and gives F1=1.0 (circular).
